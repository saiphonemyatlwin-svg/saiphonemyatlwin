# Myanmar (Burmese-speaking) Market Context for English, IELTS and Leadership Course Ads (as of Nov 2026)

Scope note: ~15 tool calls were used. Platform-reach and IELTS-fee data come from primary sources and are well grounded. Data on individual creators, view counts and price points is thin; the Gaps sections say where. Political content is limited to facts that affect advertising and demand, from the sources cited.

## 1. Platform usage, connectivity and ad/payment logistics (2025–2026)

### Takeaway
By late 2026, TikTok has the largest ad-reachable adult audience in Myanmar (32.2M, +53% YoY). Facebook's reported ad reach fell to 11.1M (−19%), but Facebook and Messenger are still the main places where people buy. Since 2021, Facebook has been officially blocked, and in 2024–25 the authorities also restricted VPNs, so in-country reach is volatile. Meta ads for Myanmar are paid only by international card or PayPal. Local wallets (KBZPay, WavePay) are used to collect course fees from customers, not to pay Meta.

### Cited Findings
**DataReportal Digital 2027: Myanmar (published 5 Nov 2026; data to Oct 2026)**
- Population 55.3M. Internet users 40.0M (72.4% penetration, +2.4% YoY). Social media user identities 32.2M (58.2% of population, +53.4% / +11M YoY). 81.5% of adults 18+ use social media. 80.3% of internet users use at least one social platform. — [DataReportal Digital 2027 Myanmar](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-myanmar)
- Ad reach, late 2026: **TikTok 32.2M adults 18+ (+53.4%, +11.2M YoY)**; **Facebook 11.1M (−19.0%, −2.6M Oct 2025→Oct 2026)**; LinkedIn 1.40M (+27.3%); Instagram 617K (−33.6%); Threads 101K. — [DataReportal Digital 2027 Myanmar](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-myanmar)
- Audience gender split, 2026: TikTok adult ad audience 54.5% female; Facebook 50.4% female. — [DataReportal Digital 2027 Myanmar](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-myanmar). The previous year's report showed a very different split: TikTok was 38.8% female / 61.2% male in late 2025. — [DataReportal Digital 2026 Myanmar](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-myanmar). DataReportal warns that source revisions make year-on-year comparisons unreliable, so treat gender trends with caution.
- 62.5M cellular connections (113% of population). Median fixed download speed 24.64 Mbps. — [DataReportal 2027](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-myanmar)
- Caveat: Meta has changed how it reports ad reach. "Negative values may indicate source corrections, and may not represent decreases in adoption." — [DataReportal 2027](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-myanmar)
- Baseline from the Digital 2026 report (Oct 2025): Facebook ad reach 13.7M (+7.9% YoY); TikTok 21.0M; Messenger 8.20M; Instagram 929K; LinkedIn 1.10M; internet users 39.8M (72.5%); social media identities 21.0M; 96.8% of mobile connections are broadband. — [DataReportal Digital 2026 Myanmar](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-myanmar)
- Neither DataReportal report lists a YouTube ad-reach figure for Myanmar. — [DataReportal 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-myanmar), [2027](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-myanmar)

**Shutdowns, blocks and VPN restrictions**
- By 4 Feb 2021, all major Myanmar ISPs had blocked Facebook on the military authorities' orders. — [Reuters graphic](https://www.reuters.com/graphics/MYANMAR-POLITICS/INTERNET-RESTRICTION/rlgpdbreepo/)
- VPN use for Facebook was blocked from 30 May 2024. Across 300 of the most important Myanmar Facebook pages, public interactions fell by about 30% on average, an estimated loss of 53M interactions between June and August 2024. — [Human Rights Myanmar, "The Great Firewall of Myanmar"](https://humanrightsmyanmar.org/the-great-firewall-of-myanmar/); see also [Global Voices Advox](https://advox.globalvoices.org/2024/06/28/war-on-citizens-how-the-juntas-vpn-ban-is-strangling-communication-in-myanmar/)
- A Cybersecurity Law that bans unauthorized VPN use was activated on 2 January 2025, according to The Irrawaddy's Facebook post. — [The Irrawaddy (Facebook post)](https://www.facebook.com/theirrawaddy/posts/junta-activates-cybersecurity-law-banning-vpnsjanuary-2-2025the-military-regime-/1026275469527610/)

**Paying for ads and collecting payment**
- Meta's Myanmar billing page lists only credit cards or co-branded debit cards (Amex, Mastercard, Visa) and PayPal as accepted payment methods. No local wallet or manual/bank method is listed. — [Meta Business Help Center – Myanmar](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1894619360792030)
- The typical Myanmar Facebook funnel runs ad or post → customer messages the Page on Messenger ("How much?") → sales team builds rapport, confirms, and sends payment details. Customers pay "usually via mobile wallets like KBZPay, Wave Pay, or cash on delivery." Facebook Live, short smartphone videos, memes and trend-jacking work well. Social proof (followers, reviews, responsiveness badge) matters because of past scams. Ad copy should mainly be in Burmese: formal for B2B, slang for youth. CPC and CPM are low compared with Western markets. — [Lwin Agency blog, Win Than Lwin, 26 Aug 2026](https://www.lwinagency.com/en/blog/why-facebook-marketing-is-important-for-business-in-myanmar)

### Inferences
- TikTok now has the largest adult ad audience, about 3x Facebook's reported reach. Facebook's reported reach is probably understated, because many in-country users connect through VPNs or appear as located outside Myanmar. Diaspora users in Thailand, Japan and elsewhere are counted in their host countries.
- To reach Burmese speakers, ads should target the Burmese language plus several countries (Myanmar, Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore, Japan), not Myanmar geography alone.
- In-country advertisers usually need a foreign card, an agency, or a diaspora partner to pay Meta. A common workaround is a local agency or reseller that pays in MMK, but this was not verified.
- Messenger and other DMs remain the place where sales close. Click-to-Messenger ads fit local buying habits better than landing pages.
- Volatile connectivity favors short, low-bandwidth videos with Burmese captions, so viewers can follow on mute or on slow connections.

### Gaps
- No verified figure for YouTube ad reach or usage in Myanmar for 2025–26. Whether Google Ads or YouTube monetization serves Myanmar under sanctions was not confirmed.
- TikTok ad-account and payment availability for Myanmar-based advertisers was not checked.
- No sourced CPM or CPC benchmarks for Myanmar.
- No primary-source confirmation of the 2025 Cybersecurity Law VPN clause; the citation is The Irrawaddy's own Facebook post. Telegram and Viber usage share for Myanmar was not found.

## 2. Demand drivers: emigration, study/work abroad, IELTS availability

### Takeaway
Since the People's Military Service Law was activated in Feb 2024, many young people have tried to leave Myanmar. Thailand is the largest destination. Japan is the fastest-growing formal pathway: Myanmar nationals there rose 126% between 2023 and 2025. IELTS is available from the British Council in Yangon and Mandalay and costs USD 190–235. Since 20 Nov 2025 it can only be paid online in USD by international card, which adds cost and payment friction for test-takers.

### Cited Findings
- The conscription law covers men aged 18–35 and women aged 18–27. Plans called for 5,000 recruits a month (60,000 a year). — [East Asia Forum, Soe Nandar Linn, 27 Oct 2024](https://eastasiaforum.org/2024/10/26/myanmar-faces-manifold-crises-as-military-conscription-drives-mass-exodus/); [PBS/AP](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/myanmar-says-newly-activated-conscription-law-will-draft-5000-people-a-month-some-think-of-fleeing)
- Young people scrambled to make "hasty exit strategies" after the activation. — [CNN, 22 Feb 2024](https://www.cnn.com/2024/02/22/asia/myanmar-mandatory-conscription-fears-young-people-intl-hnk-dst)
- About 2.3M Myanmar migrants were registered in Thailand by July 2024, with an estimated 2M more undocumented. 7,000 people queued at the Thai Embassy in Yangon for visas. Thailand denies passport renewal to people on non-ED (language-school) visas. Workers abroad pay an additional 10% tax on foreign income and must remit 25% of earnings through official banks. The article also notes poverty nearly doubled (27% in 2017 to almost half in 2023) and describes the outflow as brain drain. — [East Asia Forum](https://eastasiaforum.org/2024/10/26/myanmar-faces-manifold-crises-as-military-conscription-drives-mass-exodus/)
- A Danish Immigration Service report (Nov 2025) covers military service, exit and return. Its summary says "a large number of individuals" have tried to leave since the law was activated in early 2024. — [Danish Immigration Service (us.dk) PDF](https://us.dk/media/2f2h0pks/report-myanmar-military-service-exit-return-and-rohingya-07112025.pdf)
- **Japan:** the number of Myanmar nationals in the cited e-Stat category rose from 15,983 (2023) to 36,113 (2025), +126%. — [Lowy Institute, The Interpreter](https://www.lowyinstitute.org/the-interpreter/japan-needs-to-separate-protection-from-migration-for-myanmar). Myanmar made up about 7% of foreign workers in Japan in 2024, about 10% of Specified Skilled Worker (SSW) holders, and about 3% of Engineer/Humanities/International Services visa holders. — [IOM Japan labour migration profile, Oct 2025](https://asiapacific.iom.int/sites/g/files/tmzbdl671/files/documents/2025-10/japan_labour-migration_profile_october-2025-1.pdf). Departures of Myanmar workers to Japan in 2022 were more than 55 times the 2021 level. — [Rangsit Univ. JCSH case study (ResearchGate)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/392603825_Myanmar_Migrant_Workers_in_Japan_A_Case_Study_of_Specified_Skilled_Workers_SSW). The Myanmar population in Japan, including students, grew nearly 60% in a year. — [Nikkei Asia](https://asia.nikkei.com/spotlight/japan-immigration/myanmar-workers-head-to-japan-as-turmoil-deepens-at-home)
- **IELTS in Myanmar (British Council):** tests are held in Yangon and Mandalay, plus IELTS Online from home. Fees: Computer-delivered Academic/GT USD 210; IELTS Online USD 190; UKVI USD 235; UKVI Life Skills USD 160. Since 20 Nov 2025 (11 PM MMT), bank transfer and POS payments are no longer accepted; payment is online in USD only, by an international card (Visa/Mastercard/JCB). From 28 June 2026, all IELTS tests are computer-delivered. — [British Council Myanmar – dates, fees, locations](https://www.britishcouncil.org.mm/exam/ielts/dates-fees-locations). The Junction City (Yangon) centre lists a USD 235 fee (UKVI). — [IELTS.org test centre listing](https://ielts.org/test-centres/british-council-junction-city)

### Inferences
- The main buyer for English and IELTS courses is a 17–35-year-old planning to leave the country. Their goals include an Australia/UK student visa (IELTS), Thai jobs or study, or Japan (SSW, where Japanese is required but English helps for professional tracks). "English as a way out / career insurance" is the strongest emotional driver.
- Japan's growth suggests bundled or adjacent offers (Japanese plus English, or interview English for Japan-based employers) could do well with diaspora audiences.
- An IELTS fee of USD 190–235, payable only by international card, is a large outlay in MMK terms. Prep providers could reduce friction by explaining how to book and pay (card access, agents), or by targeting diaspora members who already have cards. Copy should frame course fees as small next to the cost of retaking the test.
- Diaspora groups in Thailand (millions, many undocumented or low-wage) and in Japan (workers and students) differ in income and goals. They need separate creatives: survival or workplace English for Thailand, professional and leadership content for Japan and Singapore.

### Gaps
- No 2025–26 counts of Myanmar IELTS test-takers or Myanmar enrolments in the UK or Australia were found.
- IDP IELTS availability in Myanmar was not confirmed.
- Singapore and Malaysia Burmese diaspora size was not sourced.
- Exit restrictions on draft-age men leaving Myanmar (reported in news) were not verified in this research.

## 4. Cultural considerations and 5. Price points (combined: limited sourced evidence)

### Takeaway
Sourced evidence covers language (Burmese first), trust and social proof (made more important by past scams), and Messenger plus mobile-wallet checkout. The research found no sourced data on MMK course prices. The Buddhist values, hierarchy and face-saving points below are inferences or general knowledge, not research findings.

### Cited Findings
- Consumers rely on social proof (followers, engagement, reviews, responsiveness badge) because of online scams. Facebook Live builds trust through unedited demonstration. Burmese-language copy is preferred, formal for B2B and casual or slangy for youth. Checkout happens through Messenger with KBZPay, WavePay or COD. — [Lwin Agency, Aug 2026](https://www.lwinagency.com/en/blog/why-facebook-marketing-is-important-for-business-in-myanmar)
- Free classes and free YouTube courses are common entry offers, as listed in section 3. — [Naomi English Online Academy](https://www.facebook.com/61594021755348/videos/1129601939784319/); [TikTok @may_georgee](https://www.tiktok.com/@may_georgee/video/7490467573394459959)
- Nearly half the population lived below the national poverty line in 2023, up from 27% in 2017, which points to strong price sensitivity. — [East Asia Forum](https://eastasiaforum.org/2024/10/26/myanmar-faces-manifold-crises-as-military-conscription-drives-mass-exodus/)
- International tutoring marketplaces (Burmese-language tutors teaching foreigners) price at USD 8–23 an hour. This is not a direct benchmark for English courses sold to Burmese learners. — [Preply](https://preply.com/en/online/burmese-tutors); [italki](https://www.italki.com/en/teachers/burmese)

### Inferences
- **Respect and hierarchy:** honorifics for teachers (ဆရာ/ဆရာမ) and an elder-mentor tone fit the culture better than brash Western "guru" hype. In practice, some local coaches do use "No.1" and "Millionaire" framing.
- **Family obligation:** "support your family", "your parents' sacrifice" and remittance framing are likely strong motives, given mandatory 25% remittance rules and the scale of migration.
- **Saving face:** ads should avoid publicly shaming poor English. "Speak without fear or embarrassment" framing and private DM onboarding probably work better.
- **Political neutrality:** given conscription and censorship, keep ads neutral. Use "future/career/abroad" framing and avoid explicit "escape the draft" messaging, which could create risk for the advertiser or for buyers.
- **Pricing:** use MMK for in-country buyers and THB, JPY or SGD for diaspora buyers. Offer low-ticket entry products, installment payments and free trials. Accept KBZPay or WavePay screenshots via Messenger, Viber or Telegram. Diaspora buyers can probably pay higher prices in local currency.
- **Trust signals:** student score screenshots (IELTS band results), video testimonials, live Q&A, the number of students taught, and a visible teacher face.

### Gaps
- No sourced MMK price ranges for online English, IELTS or leadership courses in 2025–26, and no current MMK/USD rate was gathered.
- No sourced research on Viber or Telegram usage share in Myanmar.
- No academic or market-research source on Burmese advertising culture (humor, Buddhist values) was retrieved. These points remain inferences.

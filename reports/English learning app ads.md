# Winning English-App Ads Fix One Sentence

The English-learning ads that outperformed between 2023 and November 2026 fall into two groups. One group shows a learner's mistake being fixed on screen within the first few seconds. The other makes a single blunt promise: a time box, a price anchor or a "speak, don't study grammar" claim. Platforms then reward each group very differently. The single most consequential creative is Praktika's "Gustavo" correction Short ("I have 22." / "Do you mean I am 22?"). It holds **7.4M views on a 9.37K-subscriber YouTube channel** with a 1.8% like rate, which marks it as organic, and the CEO credits this video format with taking the company from **about $1M to $10–12M ARR in one month** ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/_qQhFyDqFXI); [Sub Club](https://subclub.com/episode/how-praktika-built-a-profitable-50m-arr-ai-business-adam-turaev-praktika)). Each platform has its own kind of outlier. On **YouTube**, paid outliers are 15–36-second tutor-brand spots such as Preply's "Better Duo" (**171.7M views, ~170,000× channel median**), and organic outliers are micro-lesson Shorts such as ELSA's 31M-view pronunciation clip. On **TikTok Creative Center**, regional course brands outrank the global AI apps, led by KaleAnders' "learn English in 3 months without grammar" (**1.53M likes**) and EF Indonesia's "only 50K rupiah a month" (**up to 305,718 likes across 11+ cuts**). On **Meta**, the outliers are evergreen copy templates cloned across languages: Falou's account-wide median ad runs **333 days against a 44-day category median**, and Praktika's main page holds **50,001+ ads**, which corrects an earlier "22 ads" reading. In Southeast Asia, the longest runners are tutoring, kids' and local-school pages that sell through click-to-Messenger, and in Burmese **no international English app runs localized ads**. Local schools such as GYS (764 days) and LEL School (743 days) hold that space. For a Burmese-speaking seller, the evidence points to one playbook. Use correction and pronunciation micro-lessons and price-anchor cuts on TikTok for reach. Use evergreen Burmese emoji-bullet copy plus click-to-Messenger on Facebook to close. Frame everything around jobs, confidence and work abroad. Every ranking below rests on proxies (views, likes, run length, variant counts), not disclosed spend or ROAS.

Three conclusions follow from the table. First, **every A-grade outlier with a large multiple is either a micro-lesson or a one-line promise**. None of them is a feature tour. Second, the brands with the biggest numbers are not the ones Western growth writing discusses most. KaleAnders, EF Indonesia and Kru Pe Ann outscore every global AI app in TikTok Creative Center, and Engoo and GYS outlast them on Meta in Asia. Third, Babbel's "3 Weeks To Learn A New Language... Challenge Accepted" is the category's longest-running copy at **1,187 days** ([AdManage](https://admanage.ai/ad-library/babbel)). It is left out of the main ranking because its copy sells languages generically rather than English, but it confirms that **time-boxed challenge promises survive the longest on Meta**.

## Meta: winners are templates left running, not single ads

On Meta, winning is measured by survival, and the survivors are copy templates. Falou is the clearest case. Its account shows **7,761 ads, 381 live, and a median run of 333 days**, against a 95-advertiser language-learning category median of **44 days** (longest 686 days, Umi) ([Sprites: Falou](https://www.sprites.ai/falou-ads); [Sprites: Language learning ads](https://www.sprites.ai/language-learning-ads)). Falou reaches that median by cloning one AI-conversation video into roughly 30 language variants and never switching them off. Praktika wins on volume instead. Its "⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ Try Now" emoji-bullet block runs in English, Japanese, Korean, French, Portuguese, Spanish, Russian and German, and the brand runs dedicated local pages for Spanish and French ([Sprites: Praktika](https://www.sprites.ai/praktika-ads)). Across the category, **70% of ads are video and 36% use "Install now"** ([Sprites](https://www.sprites.ai/language-learning-ads)). Statics still carry offers, though. Cambly is **80% static** and Babbel's live ads are **79% image** ([Sprites: Cambly](https://www.sprites.ai/cambly-ads); [AdManage: Babbel](https://admanage.ai/ad-library/babbel)).

**Reconciling Praktika's footprint.** The earlier research recorded "22 ads, 15 running, median 48 days" for Praktika. That figure came from a secondary page, "Praktika.ai" (1235858642949786) ([Sprites](https://www.sprites.ai/praktika-ai-ads)). The main page (104781132385804) holds **50,001+ ads with 4,123 live and a 74-day median** ([Sprites: Praktika](https://www.sprites.ai/praktika-ads)). Both readings are correct for their page, but only the main page describes the advertiser. **Praktika is among the largest Meta advertisers in the category**, not a small one that relies only on organic. The earlier inference that its breakout came from organic creator content still holds. What has changed is that Praktika has since scaled that format into one of the heaviest paid programs in the category. Volume counts also differ by tool. AdManage reports Speak at 2,877 ads with 1,855 live ([AdManage: Speak](https://admanage.ai/ad-library/speak)), while Sprites shows 2,752 ads with 370 live ([Sprites: Speak](https://www.sprites.ai/speak-ads)). For Preply, AdManage shows 7,846 ads since Feb 2025 ([AdManage: Preply](https://admanage.ai/ad-library/preply)) and Sprites shows 10,419 ads with 2,106 live. The snapshot dates and counting rules differ, so treat these as orders of magnitude.

## TikTok: regional course brands with one blunt promise beat global AI apps

TikTok's Creative Center Top Ads paints a different picture from Meta. The highest-liked English-learning ads come from **regional course brands that make one blunt promise**. KaleAnders sells to Spanish speakers with a time box and a "no grammar" claim, EF Indonesia sells a monthly price, and Thailand's Kru Pe Ann sells celebrity entertainment. Keyword searches for Praktika, Speak, Duolingo, ELSA, Cambly, Babbel, Preply, Promova and Talkpal returned **no authorized Top Ads**. Among the AI-tutor apps, only Learna (Arabic and Turkish) and Busuu appear. Top Ads only lists ads whose advertisers have authorized them ([About Top Ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/top-ads)), so a missing brand does not mean that brand is not advertising. Praktika, Speak and Duolingo appear to grow on TikTok through organic and creator content instead.

**Reconciling the "ctr" field.** The Creative Center records retrieved through AnyAPI include a `ctr` value between 0 and 1. The earlier notes treated it as a percentile score without stating its direction. Across about 120 records, the ads with the most likes consistently carry low values (0.03–0.27), and ads with few likes carry high values (0.7–0.98). That pattern fits TikTok's on-screen "Top X% CTR" badge, where **lower means stronger**, so 0.17 ≈ top 17%. This reading is an inference and has not been confirmed. This report therefore uses **likes as the primary metric** and treats `ctr` as directional only. The `cost` field (0/1/2) looks like a budget tier but is also unconfirmed. Ad creation dates were decoded from the ad IDs, because the upper 32 bits of each ID are a Unix timestamp. The provider returned no region, so every market listed below is inferred from language and brand. Small differences between note sets, such as EF's 16s vs 17s or Kru Pe Ann's 118s vs 119s, are rounding differences in the same ads.

## YouTube: paid brand spots and organic micro-lesson Shorts are two separate games

Brand channels on YouTube split into two kinds of outlier, and the like-to-view ratio tells them apart. Organic YouTube videos usually draw roughly 1–4% likes per view. Paid in-stream views almost never produce likes, so a ratio below ~0.05% points to bought reach. **Preply is the category's heaviest YouTube buyer.** Its channel has 67.4K subscribers and a latest-30 median of about **1,000 views**, yet its 15–36-second "Better Duo" and "Progress takes two" spots carry 10M–172M views each with almost no likes ([channel](https://www.youtube.com/@preply)). Its 15–35s ad cuts get 1.4M–29M views while ordinary uploads in the same feed get a few hundred, for example [U1NEA3n49Kw](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U1NEA3n49Kw) at 1.79M sits next to [bjrOhaNiwYw](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bjrOhaNiwYw) at 593. Google's Ads Transparency Center lists **~1K video ads** for preply.com ([Ads Transparency](https://adstransparency.google.com/?region=anywhere&domain=preply.com&format=VIDEO)). These run through "Preply Inc" with creatives such as [CR09188199527465615361](https://adstransparency.google.com/advertiser/AR14659449058922332161/creative/CR09188199527465615361?region=anywhere&format=VIDEO), [CR07269103136252362753](https://adstransparency.google.com/advertiser/AR14659449058922332161/creative/CR07269103136252362753?region=anywhere&format=VIDEO) and [CR03399666631410974721](https://adstransparency.google.com/advertiser/AR14659449058922332161/creative/CR03399666631410974721?region=anywhere&format=VIDEO). Those creatives' video IDs do not appear among the public uploads, so they are probably unlisted. A second verified advertiser, an individual, also points to preply.com ([CR15923758006320234497](https://adstransparency.google.com/advertiser/AR10241869500412592129/creative/CR15923758006320234497?region=anywhere&format=VIDEO)). Talkpal follows the same playbook on a 4.34K-subscriber channel whose top-30 median is 252K views, which means the whole channel is ad inventory ([channel](https://www.youtube.com/@Talkpal)).

The AI-tutor upstarts that broke out did it with **organic Shorts**. Praktika's correction skit has a 1.8% like rate and ELSA's pronunciation Short has 1.06%. Both deliver a micro-lesson in the first three seconds and need no brand context. Every brand video checked returned `is_unlisted: false`.

**Reconciling the Gustavo video.** The earlier research could not find the Gustavo video and guessed it dated from 2023. The YouTube Short "Praktika AI Tutor Corrects Mistake Live!" (published 23 Jan 2024, 0:36) contains the line "My name is Gustavo" and the "AI tutor is much cheaper than a human tutor" price turn. That matches the CEO's description of a coach "correcting mistakes in a little conversation" and the Brazil price positioning ([Sub Club](https://subclub.com/episode/how-praktika-built-a-profitable-50m-arr-ai-business-adam-turaev-praktika); [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/adam-turaev-praktika-sub-club-podcast-2026)). It also fits Praktika's reported **20×+ revenue growth from 2023 to 2024** ([Amplitude](https://amplitude.com/blog/praktika-experimentation-and-revenue-growth)). The views differ, though: the CEO says ~10M on YouTube and the Short shows 7.4M. Reposts or other cuts may account for the gap. The match is strong but unconfirmed, so treat this Short as the best available stand-in for the breakout creative.

## Asia and Myanmar: Messenger-closing schools outlast the global apps

The longest-running localized English ads in Asia come from **tutoring, kids' and exam brands with a click-to-Messenger funnel**, not from the famous AI apps. Engoo Thailand's image ad has run **799 days** and Monkey Junior Vietnam's **470 days**. In Myanmar, Burmese schools GYS and LEL School have run **764 and 743 days**. App-install brands work differently. ELSA Vietnam rotates KOL (influencer) creatives and flash-sale prices every 1–4 weeks, so its "outlier" is template volume, not the lifespan of any single ad. The Burmese long-runners share one recipe. Each opens with a **pain question in Burmese**, such as "still hard?", "keep forgetting vocabulary?" or "afraid to speak in public?". The body mixes in English skill terms ("4 skills", "Speaking", "Vocabulary"), lists benefits at length, and ends with an **m.me or Telegram CTA**. Offers avoid card subscriptions and use one-time purchases, lifetime access and early-bird group discounts instead. Organic Burmese English pages use the same lead-magnet logic: "Myanmar Teacher" with about 83K followers ([Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/TheMyanmarTeacher/)), Naomi English's free beginner course on YouTube ([Reel](https://www.facebook.com/61594021755348/videos/1129601939784319/)), Smart English Center's "English to Burmese" reels ([Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/100094429891494/posts/english-to-burmese-viralreels-smartenglishcenter-everyone-englishteacher-burmese/294441040380224/)), free classes on TikTok ([@may_georgee](https://www.tiktok.com/@may_georgee/video/7490467573394459959)) and Telegram practice groups recruited from Facebook groups ([post](https://www.facebook.com/groups/1524770741172327/posts/4430036200645752/)).

Platform reach explains why Telegram and TikTok matter so much. By late 2026, TikTok reached **32.2M Myanmar adults (+53% YoY)** while Facebook's reported ad reach fell to **11.1M (−19%)** ([DataReportal 2027](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-myanmar)). The earlier "13.7M" Facebook figure is the Oct 2025 baseline from the previous report, not a conflict ([DataReportal 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-myanmar)). DataReportal also warns that Meta's reporting changes can show up as false declines. Facebook has been blocked since Feb 2021 ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/graphics/MYANMAR-POLITICS/INTERNET-RESTRICTION/rlgpdbreepo/)). VPN use for Facebook was blocked from May 2024, cutting interactions on major pages by about 30% ([Human Rights Myanmar](https://humanrightsmyanmar.org/the-great-firewall-of-myanmar/); [Global Voices](https://advox.globalvoices.org/2024/06/28/war-on-citizens-how-the-juntas-vpn-ban-is-strangling-communication-in-myanmar/)). ## Why the winners outperformed: five mechanics and three platform rules

Five hook families recur across all platforms and markets. Each works through a different mechanism.

**Format and length follow the proof.** The highest-like English TikTok ads are either short single-promise cuts (EF at **13–24s**, Busuu at **10s**, Teuida at 15s) or teacher talking heads (KaleAnders at **50–60s**). AI-tutor demos run **47–96s** because the demo is the proof, and Learna's shortest demo was its best ([Creative Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/inspiration/topads/)). On YouTube, paid spots cluster at **15–36 seconds**. Preply's long 1:30 cuts of its own testimonial stories drew only 700–3,100 views, while the 34-second edit drew **23.9M**. Organic Shorts that win are **16–36 seconds**. On Meta, emoji-bullet copy sits on top of video, and statics carry offers and career messages: Cambly is 80% static and ELSA's Vietnamese 71-day winner was an image.

**Localization splits two ways.** AI-tutor apps such as Praktika, Falou and Talkpal translate **one winning template word for word** and leave it running. Market-focused players such as ELSA (Japan, Vietnam), Speak (France, Korea), Preply (Spanish) and the Asian schools **write native copy around local anxieties**: interviews, meetings, presentations, the child's "golden period", exam gates. Japan is where ELSA tests the most angles (interview, pronunciation, shyness, role-play, a Speech Analyzer that promises results "たった2分間の録音で", "with just a 2-minute recording"), so new angles tend to appear there first ([Sprites: ELSA](https://www.sprites.ai/elsa-speak-ads)).

The three platforms also differ in where attention is won, what counts as proof and where the sale closes.

There is a counter-argument to all of this. Run length and likes are survival and engagement signals, not profit signals. A long-running Falou template could simply be cheap to leave on, and TikTok likes can come from entertainment that never converts. Three findings cut against that worry. First, the one case with disclosed business results (Praktika) used the same correction format that dominates the proxies. Second, Preply and Talkpal keep buying the same short formats into Q3–Q4 2026, which a marketplace would not do at 10M+ views per spot if the spots lost money. Third, advertisers rarely leave Messenger-lead ads running for two years at Engoo, GYS and Monkey Junior if those ads produce no leads. The proxies are imperfect, but they point the same way. Practitioners add a warning: if an ad promises a specific outcome and the install lands in generic onboarding, "the intent signal is wasted" ([GrowthCurve](https://growthcurve.co/what-the-best-language-learning-apps-get-right-about-growth)). Praktika's CEO found the same with tone. "Unhinged avatars were going viral in upper funnel", but putting them in the product upset users, because "we're not serving AI companions, we're serving AI coaches" ([Sub Club](https://subclub.com/episode/how-praktika-built-a-profitable-50m-arr-ai-business-adam-turaev-praktika)).

## Playbook for a Burmese-speaking English app or course seller

**Win attention on TikTok and close in Messenger.** TikTok reaches about three times as many Myanmar adults as Facebook and needs no VPN, so it should be the reach and creative-testing engine. Facebook and Messenger remain the trust layer and the checkout, as they are for every long-running Burmese and Southeast Asian advertiser found. Target by **Burmese language across Myanmar, Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore and Japan**, because diaspora users are counted in their host countries. The one genuine whitespace is a **Burmese-language AI or app product ad**. Local schools own Burmese Messenger-lead ads, and international apps run only English copy into Myanmar, so a Burmese "AI tutor corrects you" ad currently has no direct competitor.

**Copy the mechanics, not the claims.** Keep the correction and pronunciation micro-lessons, the time box ("10 minutes a day") and the price anchor. Drop the outcome guarantees. KaleAnders' "devolveremos el 100%" and the Vietnamese centres' "CAM KẾT 7.0+" sit badly with TikTok's rule that ads "must not promise or exaggerate results" ([TikTok policy](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-misleading-and-false-content)). Avoid "you + negative attribute" lines such as "Is your English bad?", which Meta's personal-attributes rule targets ([Tagada](https://www.tagada.io/payments/info-products/advertising)). Write in the first person instead, as Praktika does with "I understand but I can't speak". Skip family-shame scripts such as Learna's "ashamed of her mother's English", because in a face-saving culture the comedy framing of Preply's airport skit transfers better. Keep every line within **future, job, confidence, work abroad**, and never reference politics, conscription or "escape".

**Run five distinct concepts, not thirty variants.** Andromeda rewards distinct concepts, and TikTok asks for 3–5 creatives per ad group. Rotate these concepts: (1) a correction skit, (2) a pronunciation challenge, (3) a price-anchor cut, (4) a workplace scenario for the diaspora, and (5) a Burmese emoji-bullet evergreen static. Use the offer pattern every Burmese long-runner shares: free level check → one-time or lifetime price paid by KBZPay/WavePay screenshot → onboarding into Telegram or Messenger. Price separately in THB, JPY and SGD for diaspora buyers. No sourced Myanmar CPM or CPL exists, and Southeast Asian Facebook CPMs run roughly **$1–3.40** ([Stackmatix](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/facebook-ads-cpm-by-country-2026); [Lebesgue](https://lebesgue.io/facebook-ads/facebook-cpm-by-country)), so set your own baseline in the first 2–4 weeks. Triage creatives by thumbstop first: under 20% is weak and 30%+ is strong ([Popjam](https://popjam.io/blog/thumbstop-rate)). Education lead ads convert well (15.87% CVR in the US) but have among the lowest CTRs, so **the bottleneck is the hook** ([WordStream 2026](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/facebook-ads-benchmarks-2026)).

**Copy Praktika's sourcing loop at small scale.** Recruit micro-creators with fewer than 5,000 followers, Burmese teachers and diaspora workers, for a fixed fee plus a view bonus. Do not script them. Move anything that beats 3× its account median into Spark Ads or boosted posts, and re-shoot it with other creators ([Sub Club](https://subclub.com/episode/how-praktika-built-a-profitable-50m-arr-ai-business-adam-turaev-praktika)).

## Keeping the swipe file current: method and remaining gaps

**Weekly method.** On Meta, search each competitor's Page rather than a keyword, keep the "Impressions: high to low" sort, and score each ad as **days active × number of IDs sharing the copy** ([Succession Media](https://succession.media/guides/meta-ads-library/); [Eden](https://eden.so/blog/facebook-ad-library/)). Discount "multiple versions" that come only from DCO format variants ([Sprout Social](https://support.sproutsocial.com/hc/en-us/articles/360050699272-How-do-I-identify-ads-with-multiple-versions)). Compare each ad to the brand's own median from Sprites, since 48 days is an outlier for ELSA but noise for Falou. Watch the long-runners in Burmese ("အင်္ဂလိပ်စာ", "English Speaking") and the emerging AI-tutor pages (LunaSpeak, Linguza, Fluzy, Hilingo). On TikTok Creative Center, filter Top Ads by Education, conversion or lead objectives and Vietnam/Thailand/Indonesia as Myanmar proxies. Sort by likes, then open the second-by-second retention and CVR curve ([Creative Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/inspiration/topads/pc/en); [Metricool](https://metricool.com/tiktok-creative-center/)). On YouTube, divide views by the median of the last 20–30 uploads, with **3× as worth a look and 5× as worth studying** ([1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/viral-youtube-video-ideas-outlier-method/); [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/outlier-scores/)). Check the like ratio to separate paid from organic, and query Ads Transparency by domain for unlisted creatives. Motion's hook and click scores are a useful internal lens once your own ads are live ([Motion](https://help.motionapp.com/en/articles/8991407-motion-metrics-for-meta-and-tiktok)). Log each ad with the same columns used in this report: hook, format, angle, offer, CTA, length, metric and grade.

**What remains unknown.** No public CPI, CPA or ROAS exists for any ad here, so every ranking is a proxy. The **first-three-second visuals and spoken hooks** were not verified for most Meta and TikTok videos. Only the YouTube transcripts and Praktika's Upspring hook text are verbatim speech, and everything else quotes the caption or ad copy. Meta video lengths are unknown. TikTok Creative Center returned no region or view counts, and the meaning of its `ctr` and `cost` fields is inferred. The Gustavo match is strong but unconfirmed. Firecrawl credits ran out, and admanage.ai, tiktok.com, pipiads.com and gethandler.ai were blocked, so the following were not run: TikTok searches for Speak, Falou, Loora, Memrise, Lingoda and Engoo; Ads Transparency checks for speak.com, praktika.ai, talkpal.ai, babbel.com, cambly.com, elsaspeak.com and promova.com; and AdManage variant counts for this round. Nothing was captured for Cake, Cakap, Englishdee, Engoo Japan, Ruangguru, Kampus, Langgo or Cambly Thailand. No Myanmar-targeted ad from a global app, no diaspora-targeted Burmese ad and no Myanmar CPM, CPL or MMK price data were found. The next steps are Adyntel Page-ID pulls, Meta Ad Library country filters (MM, TH, JP, SG, MY) in a live browser, and a manual review of the top 20 videos for their opening frames.

## Conclusion

English-learning ads that win across platforms do so by giving value before the pitch. The viewer either learns a correct sentence or gets one objection answered in the first few seconds, and the product appears as the obvious continuation. The bigger strategic finding is where the competition is not. The global AI-tutor apps dominate Western ad-intelligence write-ups, yet regional course brands outscore them on TikTok, Messenger-closing schools outlast them on Meta in Asia, and none of them speaks Burmese. A Burmese seller who combines Praktika's correction format, EF's one-line price anchor and the local schools' Messenger close would be entering a market where each of those pieces is already proven but nobody has assembled them in Burmese.

One caution follows from the same evidence. The formats that travel best (correction, pronunciation, the time box) are also the easiest for competitors to copy, so they work as entry tickets rather than lasting advantages. What lasted longest in this dataset was a template run patiently for hundreds of days and fed by a steady pipeline of new creators: Falou's 333-day median, Engoo's 799 days, GYS's 764. The durable advantage is the operating loop of creator sourcing, outlier detection and patient evergreen running, more than any single hook.

# Craft Standards: Visual, Audio, Edit, Text

## Shoot setup checklist
- **Format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920, 30fps (60fps if there's movement). Shoot 4K if possible to allow punch-in zooms.
- **Framing:** eyes on the upper third line, head room small, camera at eye level. Lens at arm's length or more (avoid phone-wide distortion).
- **Light:** one soft key light 45° to the side, slightly above eye level. Window light counts. Never backlit by a window.
- **Background:** depth (subject 1–2 m from the wall), one meaningful object (books, plant, gold accent), nothing busy.
- **Wardrobe:** solid colours from the brand palette. No fine stripes or logos.
- **Mic:** lav or shotgun within 30 cm. Soft room (curtains, rug) to kill echo. Record a 5-second room-tone clip.
- **Delivery:** energy at 120% of normal conversation. Look into the lens, not the screen. Start the first word on the first frame.

## Safe zone (Reels / TikTok)
Keep text and faces out of the top ~250px, the bottom ~400px and the right ~150px (UI buttons and caption).
Key text lives in the middle band.

## Edit rhythm
| Element | Standard |
|---|---|
| First cut | ≤ 1.5s, or a visible change (zoom/b-roll) inside 2s |
| Pattern interrupt | every 3–6s: jump cut, 110–120% punch-in, B-roll, graphic, angle |
| Pauses/breaths | removed; leave ~4 frames between sentences |
| B-roll | shows what is being *said* (literal) or *felt* (metaphor: iceberg, seed, bridge) |
| Graphics | one idea per graphic; frameworks build on screen step by step (saves!) |
| Ending | cut on the tattoo line; ideally it rhymes with frame 1 so the loop feels seamless |

## Audio mix
- Voice peak about −6 dBFS, loudness about −14 LUFS integrated (platform norm).
- Music −20 to −25 dB under the voice. Instrumental only. Mood: calm, cinematic, not hype.
- SFX: a soft whoosh on cuts and a subtle hit on the key line. Max 1 per 3–5 seconds.
- Denoise and de-ess the voice. Silence is better than a noisy room.

## Text on screen
- **Hook text:** ≤ 7 words, large, top-middle safe band, on screen from frame 1.
- **Captions:** burned in, 2–4 words per line, high contrast (white/cream on dark with a shadow, or gold highlight on the key word).
- **Burmese:** use a Unicode Burmese font that renders stacked characters correctly (e.g. Pyidaungsu / Noto Sans Myanmar). Check that nothing is clipped.
- One highlighted keyword per line in brand gold. Don't highlight everything.

## Common "lacking" fixes, quick reference
| Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|
| Feels slow | Cut the first sentence. Start on the second. |
| Talking head is boring | Add punch-ins every 4s + 3 B-roll inserts |
| Viewers leave mid-way | Open a loop in the first 5s; pay it off later |
| Nobody shares | Make the payoff about a *specific person/situation* someone can tag |
| Nobody saves | Put the framework on screen as a numbered build |
| Looks amateur | Light first, then audio, then the camera. In that order. |

## What works (keep / steal)
1. **Hook stack, frame 1.** A graph, a two-word label in white and yellow, and a view count all fade in within 0.3s, while a finger points up at the graph. Visual, text and spoken hooks all fire at once. Earns the **3-second hold**.
2. **One new piece of information every ~2s.** Each beat swaps the graph, the label and the view count while the shot stays the same. That works as a pattern interrupt without any editing tricks. Earns **completion**.
3. **Built-in loop.** It ends mid-sentence ("…what you should be looking for") while the goal graph is still on screen, then restarts. Viewers rewatch to study the shapes. Earns **rewatches**.
4. **Very saveable and sendable.** It's a diagnostic cheat-sheet creators will screenshot or send to a creator friend. Earns **saves and sends**.
5. **Contrast as implied proof.** View counts from 212 to 15.3M make each shape feel real and the stakes obvious.

## Dr. Sai remix (borrow the structure, not the content)
Format: **"Diagnose by shape"**. Five visuals in 10–15s, each with one diagnosis line, ending in a loop with the healthy shape.
- CEO pillar: "If your team's weekly output looks like this…" (spike-and-crash / slow decline / flat / healthy)
- Business-scaling pillar: five cash-flow chart shapes → what each one says about the business
- Personal-development pillar: five energy-across-the-day curves → which habit is broken
Add what the original lacks: a contextual credibility line, one fix per shape (or a part-2 series), and a Telegram keyword CTA.
Brand: keep the language clean (no "sh*t"), calm delivery, gold highlight on the key word, one figure, one light.
